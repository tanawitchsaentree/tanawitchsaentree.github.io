## Overview

Allianz processes thousands of incoming documents every day, from claims and invoices to legal notices and correspondence. An internal AI platform classifies each one and routes it to the right team.

This tool is the layer that controls how that AI thinks.

## The Problem

Before the platform, document triage was manual. Skilled claims handlers spent hours every day on mechanical routing: reading documents, categorizing them, and forwarding them to the right queue. The work was pure sorting, with no real analysis or decision-making involved.

When the AI was introduced, the problem didn't disappear. It changed shape instead. The AI was fast, but when it was wrong, no one knew until something downstream broke. And when it was right, handlers still double-checked everything, because there was no way to know which result to trust.

**The system had volume without confidence.**

## Key Insight

Users don't fear AI being wrong. They fear being wrong without knowing it.

The goal was to make the AI's uncertainty legible, so configurators could act on it instead of being surprised by it.

## What Changed

Configurators who'd stopped touching prompts for weeks began publishing new versions daily.
