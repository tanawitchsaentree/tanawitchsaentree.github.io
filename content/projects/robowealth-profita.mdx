## Profita

I worked on Profita at Robowealth in 2020 as a Senior UX/UI Designer. The app combines fund browsing, Robo Advisor plans and portfolio information for LH Bank customers.

The current case study covers fund details, purchase amounts, order review and account screens.

[Read the case study](/projects/profita/)

## Project credit

My contribution was in 2020. In 2023, LH Bank received Highly Commended for Best App for Customer Experience at the Retail Banker International Asia Trailblazer Awards. The app continued to develop between those dates.
